TRIZ Principle 1 of 40

Cut it into pieces

Segmentation: when one solid object can't do two jobs at once, split it into parts.

1A Split into separate parts chocolate bar, train cars
1B Easy to take apart building bricks, phone case
1C Go finer and finer bike chain, sand, spray
Your turn
Your school bag is too big on light days and too small on project days.

How would you segment it? Think first, then tap "Show ideas".

Step 1 / 6

  • Zip-off pockets: add a module on project days, remove it on light days (1B).
  • Two bags that clip together into one big bag (1A).
  • Accordion sections that fold flat and expand when needed (1A + hinges).

Spot it in the wild: pizza slices, a folding phone, train cars, a playlist split into tracks. Each one turns one big thing into many small things that work together.
